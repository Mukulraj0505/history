Prelims Trap Warning for Ancient History

UPSC Terminology Trap: In ancient history questions, UPSC frequently tests administrative, social, and economic terms (e.g., Vishti = forced labour, Kulyavapa/Dronavapa = land measurements, Eripatti = land donated for tank maintenance). Always compile a dedicated running gloss of terms.
