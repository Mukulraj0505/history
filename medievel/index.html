Medieval Indian History

Satish Chandra (History of Medieval India) + NCERT Class VII & XII (Themes II)

Medieval History Strategy for UPSC

Weightage Insight: Medieval History contributes 3 to 5 questions in Prelims with high predictability around Delhi Sultanate administrative terms, Vijayanagara kingdom (institutions, travelers, architecture), Bhakti/Sufi saint lineages, and Mughal agrarian systems (Mansabdari, Dahsala, Jagirdari).
Primary Source Satish Chandra (Orientation BlackSwan) / NCERT
High Yield Areas Vijayanagara, Mansabdari system, Bhakti/Sufi saints
Foreign Travellers Ibn Battuta, Abdur Razzaq, Bernier, Tavernier, Manucci
PYQ Question Pattern Administrative offices (Diwan-i-Arz, Barid), Land revenue (Iqta)
