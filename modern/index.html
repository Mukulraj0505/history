Modern History Strategy for UPSC

Weightage Insight: Modern Indian History forms the largest chunk of historical questions in UPSC Prelims (typically 6–10 questions) and is directly mapped to GS Paper-I syllabus: "Modern Indian history from about the middle of the eighteenth century until the present- significant events, personalities, issues."
Primary Source Spectrum — A Brief History of Modern India
Value Addition NCERT Class XII (Themes in Indian History III)
Mains Focus Socio-religious reform, Freedom Struggle phases, Subaltern views
Prelims Focus Acts, Chronology, Governors-General, Tribal/Peasant revolts
